## What changed

The operating scope changed, rather than only the revenue target. Direct consumer campaigns were removed. Paid operating FTEs are 3 / 4.5 / 5.5; development is a bounded six-month MVP. Subscription tiers and eight distinct workflows per organization-month replace the previous low-usage proposition. No existing customer evidence was changed. Historical deliverables remain in private backups and Git history.

Proposed Lite/Pro prices are 49,000/99,000 KZT monthly including VAT, plus transaction fees. No direct consumer advertising. B2B outreach budgets 2.4/4.8/4.8m at 5,000 per qualified organization; capacity 40 qualified organizations per sales FTE-month; 25% trial and 40% paid conversion, two-month lag, 2% monthly churn, eight funded workflows per account-month. All are hypotheses. Astana pilot followed by conditional wider Kazakhstan coverage; no measured eligible-agency count.

Additional input diagnostics: subscription prices −20% and four workflows/account-month give NPV -20.18m; conversion +10% gives 15.74m. These are scenario calculations, not probability-weighted estimates. The live workbook supports changing the relevant inputs.

Role capacity is checked monthly: 1 / 1.5 / 2 service FTE × 800 workflows/month, approximately 10 minutes each plus account support. No free founder labour. B2B CAC includes paid sales payroll; the 6k/account support allocation is within payroll. LTV is capped at 36 months, excludes discounting and workflow income. Cash funding uses the monthly trough, not only year-end balances. Negative balances are unmet funding, not debt.

Profit is a conditional forecast, not actual earnings or validated demand. Lower conversion or higher costs can make NPV negative. No survey responses, paying clients, bank agreement or production Besu deployment are claimed. Paid founder and staff costs, tax, bank fees, security and administrative allowances are included. Receipts occur in the service month; annual CIT is allocated evenly monthly for liquidity planning, not a statutory tax schedule.

## Launch gates

Initial two-month 5.6m ceiling: payroll 2.7m, bank/legal 2m, tools 0.3m, discovery 0.6m. Included within 18m setup. Interview at least 20 eligible organizations with consent, preserve refusals, and validate the prospect population. Stated intent does not prove paid retention. No real paid trial occurs in the course.

Before commercial scaling, require costed bank/security feasibility, full-fee acceptance, 10% qualified-to-paid, eight funded workflows/account-month, churn <=2%, subscription CAC payback <=12 months and finite LTV/CAC >=3. These are management thresholds, not achieved outcomes. Stop or reforecast if gates fail. 40m is not a commitment to spend everything.
