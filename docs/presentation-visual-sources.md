The university logo is retained from the supplied project materials on the title slide only. All graphs, process diagrams, tables and calculations are project-authored. The numbered figures and tables cite their sources. The revenue/opex chart and financial tables remain editable in PowerPoint.

The deck keeps 23 slides and the existing topic assignments: slides 1–12 cover the project, 13 is Conclusion, 14 is AI Disclosure Statement, 15 is References, and 16–23 are appendices. Commercial assumptions, evidence limitations and figures remain consistent with the report and financial model.
