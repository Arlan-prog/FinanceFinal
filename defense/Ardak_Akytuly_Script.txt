CORE Q&A
Q: Did you prove demand?
A: No. We corrected the method and exposed the assumptions. No customer survey or paid retention evidence exists. The recommendation is to defer full launch.
Q: Where do customers come from?
A: Year 1 direct budget 4.2m / CPL 1,500 × 30% qualified × 50% both signed × 70% funded = 294 contracts. These are hypotheses. B2B organizations use a separate funnel and monthly cohorts.
Q: Why not multiply year-end subscribers by twelve?
A: Accounts arrive during the year and some leave. Billable accounts are opening accounts after churn plus half of new accounts. We sum account-months. Year 1 average B2B is 1.76, year-end 4.12.
Q: Is housing stock your market size?
A: No. BNS reports 575,983 Astana premises for 2025. Rented share, lease turnover and eligibility are unknown. Scenario ranges are not validated TAM/SAM and do not drive sales.
Q: Why is blockchain useful, and could a database work?
A: Independent organizations could share an auditable state record. A conventional database is a valid simpler alternative if multi-party governance adds insufficient value. Blockchain cannot prove evidence is true or force a bank to pay.
Q: Where is the money?
A: At a hypothetical bank. Solidity records KZT amounts and authorizes allocations. Mock bank acknowledgement is separate from authorization. Deposits are never Kepil revenue or operating finance.
Q: What runs today?
A: Local Hardhat and Solidity. Four-validator Besu QBFT, bank API, recovery, pause and document storage are proposed. Tests do not establish their operation.
Q: Why is NPV negative and what did you change?
A: Acquisition-derived revenue is far below the retained cost envelope. We changed the sales method, not the numbers to force profitability. Financing is excluded from NPV. Study funding buys evidence, not a claim of business viability.
Q: Why 20% discount and 20% equity?
A: The discount rate is the course-compliant comparison assumption, not measured WACC. Equity is a separate classroom negotiation term, not a discount rate or NPV-based valuation.
Q: Tokenomics?
A: No token is issued. Bank-held KZT and application permissions serve the use case. No token supply, sale, staking yield or token-funded revenue is assumed.
Q: What stops automatic release?
A: A dispute blocks it. Timed release needs tenant no-claims confirmation, no dispute, deadline reached and an explicit caller transaction. Time alone never executes code.
Q: What are the main security limitations?
A: A bank assertion may be false; inspector keys may be compromised; hashes do not prove truth. Immutable roles lack recovery and pause. Role/state tests cover selected local behavior, not a security audit.
Q: How is the study controlled?
A: A separate 12m cap, no revenue assumption and no second tranche. Direct CAC must fit contribution; B2B economics and bank feasibility need evidence. Stop if the gates fail. Course interviews cannot prove paid retention.
Q: What did AI do?
A: OpenAI Codex (GPT-6) assisted research, code, financial formulas, writing and formatting. We must independently understand and review the work. No synthetic survey responses are evidence.
