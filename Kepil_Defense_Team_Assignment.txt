CORE Q&A
Q: Why is the new forecast profitable?
A: We changed scope: paid FTE 3/4.5/5.5, no consumer advertising, agency subscription and repeated distinct workflows. The result also requires unmeasured pricing, usage and conversions; we show downside cases rather than claim proven demand.

Q: How do you justify the prices?
A: 49k/99k including VAT are proposed tiers, compared only as context with company software subscriptions. Bitrix24 is a broader, different product. Interviews and later lawful paid validation must establish acceptance of both subscription and transaction fees.

Q: Where do customers come from?
A: Annual qualified organizations: 2.4m / 5,000 = 480 in Year 1. Trial 25% and paid 40% give 10% conversion, after two months: 40 new paid accounts during Year 1. Billable accounts are opening after churn plus half of new. NPV uses -18m and three after-tax cash flows. Year 3 operating break-even is 67.69 average billable accounts at the stated prices and eight monthly workflows.

Q: What is the market?
A: Astana pilot, conditional wider Kazakhstan coverage after Year 1. BNS apartment stock is not annual leases or an eligible-agency count. We must validate a deduplicated 2,400-prospect list over three years.

Q: Where is the money?
A: A hypothetical bank holds deposits. The contract authorizes allocations; mock bank settlement acknowledgement is separate. Deposits never finance Kepil.

Q: What works today?
A: Defense support. No formal oral presentation is required. Any member may be asked to explain this topic.

Our target platform is Besu. Four independent organizations would operate the network: Kepil, a bank, an inspection company, and a technical operator. We chose Besu because it supports Solidity, the language used in our prototype. With four validators, QBFT can tolerate one faulty validator under its assumptions. Fabric is an alternative, but would require a different setup. Today, only the local Hardhat prototype is implemented. Besu and the bank connection are still planned.

Q: Why blockchain?
A: Independent parties could share an auditable record. A conventional database is a valid simpler alternative if independent governance adds insufficient value.
